## Study title

The Impact of Visual Imagery on Spatial Working Memory Recall: An Online Follow-up Study

## Short description (shown in the study listing)

A computer-based memory task: you will remember positions on a grid, imagine simple patterns, and respond to short tones using the keyboard. Desktop or laptop computer required; headphones recommended, speakers acceptable. About 110 minutes. Pay: $26 ($14 per hour).

## Full description

Researchers at the University of Missouri Working Memory Lab invite you to participate in an online study investigating how people remember and mentally manipulate visual and auditory information.

**What you will do.** You will complete one session of about 110 minutes in your web browser. You will calibrate your screen with a credit card, set your sound level, remember and compare dot positions on a grid and short pure tones, imagine simple geometric patterns, and answer brief questionnaires. The session also includes a brief demographics questionnaire, instruction checks, and short questionnaires about your experience. Responses are made with the keyboard (left and right arrow keys). The study goes fullscreen: please keep this one browser tab focused for the whole session and do not change the zoom. There is nothing to install. The session includes flashing visual items and short tones that vary in pitch and loudness. Please do not take part if these could cause you discomfort (for example, photosensitive epilepsy or migraines triggered by flashing lights).

**Duration.** Approximately 110 minutes in a single session. It can take longer if the practice has to be repeated.

**Compensation.** You will be compensated at or above Prolific's recommended fair-pay rate, currently approximately $12.00 per hour: you will be paid $26 for the full session, which is about $14 per hour. Payment is made by Prolific when your submission is approved after you finish the session. If the compensation you received does not align with your expectations for this study, please contact the PI at hnth5@missouri.edu.

**Requirements.**

- Desktop or laptop computer (no mobile devices or tablets)
- Working keyboard
- Working audio output (built-in speakers, external speakers, or headphones; headphones are recommended)
- Stable internet connection
- A current desktop version of Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, or Safari (Chrome or Edge 93 or later, Firefox 92 or later, Safari 15.4 or later)
- A quiet environment free of distractions for the duration of the session
- A standard-size credit card or debit card (for screen-size calibration only; no financial information is collected)

**Who can take part (Prolific screeners).** This study is available only to participants who meet all of the following criteria, which Prolific applies before the study is shown:

- Age: 18 to 40 years
- First language: English
- Country of birth: United States
- Prolific approval rate: 85% or higher
- Hearing: no hearing loss or hearing difficulties
- Vision: normal or corrected-to-normal vision
- Cognitive health: never diagnosed with mild cognitive impairment or dementia

Eligibility is determined only by these Prolific filters, which are applied before you see this study. The study itself does not test your age, hearing, or vision, and it does not ask for your name, email address, date of birth, or any other information that identifies you, or about your health or medical conditions. Your age and sex come from the information Prolific already holds. The study runs only on a desktop or laptop computer: Prolific shows this requirement on the study page, and the first step of the study checks it. Please take part only if you can use a desktop or laptop computer with a working keyboard, a stable internet connection and working audio output, and if you can attend to short tones and to brief visual items that flash or appear and disappear quickly without adverse effects (for example, if you have photosensitive epilepsy or migraines triggered by flashing lights, please do not take part). These points describe what the task requires; the study does not check them.

**Checks that happen during the study.** The following checks are task-capability checks, not eligibility screening. They confirm that the task can be run accurately on your equipment, and most of them can end the session. They are described again in the consent form.

1. Device check (at the very start, before the consent form): the study runs only on a desktop or laptop computer. If a phone or tablet is detected, the session ends.
2. Screen-size calibration: you match an on-screen rectangle to a credit card, with up to three attempts. If a usable setting cannot be obtained, the session ends.
3. Sound check: you adjust the volume until a series of short tones is clearly and comfortably audible. If the tones cannot be made audible, the session ends.
4. Headphone check: a brief listening check, recorded only. It never ends the session. Headphones are recommended, but speakers are acceptable.
5. Pitch-discrimination check: eight pairs of tones, saying whether the second tone was higher or lower. At least 7 of 8 correct are required; one retry is allowed after raising the volume. If you score below 7 of 8 on the second attempt, the session ends.
6. Timing check: for a few seconds the page measures your display refresh rate and timer accuracy. If the computer is too slow or unsteady, you can run the check once more; if it fails again, the session ends.
7. Practice-accuracy criterion: you must reach at least 70% correct on the practice trials, with one repeat allowed, before the main task begins. If you do not, the session ends.
8. Display lock: after calibration the page must stay at the same zoom and in fullscreen. If either changes, the task pauses until you restore it, and the affected trials are excluded from analysis. If you cannot restore it, a button lets you end the session.
9. Attention checks: three brief typed instruction checks occur during the session. Please read each instruction carefully; following it passes the check.

**If a check is not passed.** If one of these checks ends your session, you are shown a message and sent back to Prolific, where your submission is recorded as screened out. You are then paid a fixed screen-out payment of $0.14 through Prolific, the same whichever of these checks ends the session.

**Declining or leaving.** Participation is voluntary. You can decline consent at the start, or stop at any time by selecting "Stop without completing" on Prolific, which returns your submission, without penalty. Data that your browser has already sent before you stop are kept; if you would like them removed, contact the Lead Researcher and give your Prolific ID.

**Payment.** $26 for completing the full session, paid by Prolific when your submission is approved. Partial payment if a check ends the session: a fixed screen-out payment of $0.14. There are no bonus payments.

**Data and privacy.** This study is pseudonymous: your responses are stored under your Prolific Participant ID only, in private research files, and the ID is replaced by a random code in the files used for analysis and sharing. There is no audio or video recording. We do not record your name, email address, or IP address in the study data. To check that the task runs accurately on your computer, we record the browser and operating system, screen and window size, display refresh rate and timer accuracy, audio output settings, and whether the page is hidden, zoomed, or leaves fullscreen. Your responses are sent over an encrypted connection to a storage folder that only the research team can access. Contact: if you have questions before, during, or after participation, please contact the Lead Researcher, Hamid Nourbakhshi, at hnth5@missouri.edu. This study has been reviewed and approved by the University of Missouri Institutional Review Board (IRB #2101803). If you have questions about your rights as a research participant, contact the IRB at 573-882-3181 or muresearchirb@missouri.edu.

## Differences from the IRB-approved recruitment script

- Title: identical.
- Duration: "approximately 110 minutes" (approved: 80 to 110 minutes).
- Compensation: states the real payment ($26, about $14 per hour) together with the approved recommended-rate wording ("approximately $12.00 per hour").
- Anonymity: "pseudonymous" replaces "anonymous survey" because responses are stored under the Prolific ID; the list of technical data recorded (browser and operating system, screen and window size, refresh rate and timing, audio settings, page visibility) is added.
- Screening: the approved script describes a demographics questionnaire (including age, hearing, vision, medication and English fluency) that also screens for eligibility. Prolific does not allow screening inside a study or stating extra screening requirements only in the description (account hold, June 2026), so this study asks no age or date of birth, ends no session on a demographic answer, and gives eligibility only through Prolific filters (age, first language, country of birth, approval rate, hearing, vision, cognitive health). The demographics questionnaire now asks handedness and highest level of education only. The approved conditions that no filter enforces (colour vision, traumatic brain injury) are not stated as requirements; only the task's technical and safety requirements are.
- Checks, screen-out payment, data storage and what happens to data when a participant stops are added; none appears in the approved script.
- The headphone requirement is not listed as a filter: speakers are acceptable.
